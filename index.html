Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The check is free and there is no obligation to buy anything.

Will I get sales calls?

Only if you ask for a quote at the end. You can see your estimate without entering any contact details. If you do ask for a quote, your details go only to the solar retailer and installer who would do the job, and you can opt out at any time. See our privacy policy.

How accurate are the numbers?

They are estimates, based on typical Victorian electricity prices and average Melbourne sunshine. Your firm price comes in a written quote after your roof and switchboard are checked.

What changes on 1 January 2027?

The federal battery discount steps down on 1 January 2027. It is based on the date the battery is installed, not the date it is ordered.

I already have solar. Can I add just a battery?

Yes. Tell the check you already have panels and it will estimate what a battery alone could save you.

Which areas do you cover?

Homes in Victoria.
