Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what ("we", "us") does with the details you give us through the solar and battery savings check. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

We only collect what you type or choose in the savings check:

  • your name, mobile number and email address, and your business name if you tell us it is for a business
  • your postcode
  • your answers about your home and electricity: whether you own or rent, whether you already have solar or a battery, your roof, your power supply, the size of your bill, when you use power, and how soon you want to go ahead
  • whether your household income is under the Solar Victoria rebate limit (a yes, no or not sure answer only; we never ask for the amount)
  • the day and time you would like a call, and anything you write in the notes box
  • the date and time you sent the form, and any campaign tag in the link you followed to reach this page

We do not ask for payment details, identity documents or copies of your electricity bill. You can use the savings check and see your estimate without giving us your name or contact details. We only receive your information when you press the button to request a call.

Why we collect it

  • to call, text or email you about the solar and battery quote you asked for
  • to prepare an estimate and quote that suits your home
  • to check which government rebates you may be able to claim
  • to arrange a site inspection and installation if you decide to go ahead
  • to keep a record of your request and of any request to stop contacting you

Who we share it with

We share your details with the solar retailer and installation partner who prepares your quote and carries out the work. They may contact you directly about your quote.

We do not sell your details, and we do not pass them to anyone else for their own marketing.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Your details are sent over an encrypted connection and kept in a private Google Sheets spreadsheet that only our sales staff can open. We also receive them by email so we can call you promptly. Google may store this data on servers outside Australia, including in the United States and Singapore.

This website is hosted by GitHub, which keeps standard technical logs of visits, such as IP addresses, for security purposes. We do not have access to those logs.

We keep your details for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any installation that follows, and for as long as the law requires us to keep business records. After that we delete them.

Cookies and tracking

This page does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

Calls, texts and emails

We only contact you if you tick the consent box and send the form. You can ask us to stop at any time by telling the person who calls you, replying STOP to a text, or using the unsubscribe option in an email. We will act on your request within five business days.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your details

You can ask us for a copy of the details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. We will respond within 30 days and there is no charge.

Questions and complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
